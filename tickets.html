A few honest answers

Frequently Asked Questions

What is included in the museum experience?

ArtSpace is an educational digital gallery concept featuring artist biographies, artwork descriptions, and creative discovery pages. It is not a real ticketed venue.

Do I need to choose a ticket type?

For this demonstration form, yes. It shows how a real booking interface could collect a visitor’s preferred admission type, but no reservation is made.

Can children visit the museum?

Child Admission is included as a fictional example category. ArtSpace welcomes curiosity at every age in its digital experience.

Is this a real museum?

No. ArtSpace is an imaginary digital art museum created as a school website-design project. It does not offer real visits, bookings, payments, or visitor services.

A fictional digital art museum designed for unhurried looking, creative learning, and the quiet thrill of finding a new favourite.
